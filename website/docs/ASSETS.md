# Images, prompts and provenance

Created using the **built-in image generation tool**, then optimized with Sharp. No runtime image API or API key is used. Original PNGs are retained in `assets/source/`; production WebP files are in `public/images/`.

## Hero

Source: `assets/source/meaningful-gifts-hero.png`
Production: `public/images/meaningful-gifts-hero.webp` (about 160 KB).

Prompt: Premium editorial marketing illustration, wide before-and-after scene. Left 40 percent: unwanted decorative vases, clocks, duplicate ornaments and gift boxes collecting dust, muted lavender. Right 60 percent: happy South Asian family, mother, father and child, enjoying a sage sofa and an oak dining table they actually wished for. Matte dimensional clay and paper texture with warm natural light, cream, violet, coral and mint palette, generous crop margins. Emotional, sophisticated, spacious. No text, logos or lettering.

## Wishlist atlas

Source: `assets/source/wishlist-products.png`
Production: `public/images/wishlist-products.webp` (about 104 KB).

Prompt: Premium product illustration atlas, exactly three columns and two rows of equal square cells on warm off-white #F8F7F3. Top row: oak round dining table and chairs; mint mixer grinder with steel jars; silver laptop with blank lavender screen. Bottom row: sage sofa; wooden baby crib with cream bedding; tropical island vacation with coral suitcase and small airplane. Consistent matte dimensional product rendering, soft shadows, generous cell margins, no text or logos.
